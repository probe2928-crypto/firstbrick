# LinkedIn — founding announcement (post from the Oriel Foundation page, signed Scott Park)

## English (post this one)

I've volunteered abroad for years, in Thailand in 2020 and in Congo in 2025. Those trips taught me that steady, small help beats a big one-off gift, and that people who give deserve to see where every dollar goes. Today I'm starting something built on that.

Oriel Foundation is a foundation in the making for families in need and their lovely children. The plan is simple and slow on purpose:

1. Learn — tutoring, books and scholarships, run with local teachers.
2. Live — meals, clean water, health checks, a safe way to school, and living support for families having the hardest time.
3. Build — classrooms, libraries and, when a community asks for it, a school.

Why on-chain? Because blockchains are good at two things charity often struggles with: moving money across borders cheaply, and keeping records anyone can check. Every gift to Oriel will be traceable on a public blockchain, with a receipt for every payout.

Why "Oriel"? An oriel is a window that leans out from a wall to catch more light. That's the job: more light for children's learning, and clear glass so anyone can see where every gift goes. In Chinese we are 明窗基金會.

What we will not do: orphanage visits, short trips to play with kids, or photos without a guardian's consent.

We are not taking donations yet. Registration comes first, and I'll share every step here, including what goes wrong.

Between now and January I'm looking for:
• a first local partner already working with families in need and their children
• advisers in education, nonprofit law and accounting
• people who want to join the founding circle

If that's you, or you know who it is, the website is below. I'd be grateful for a comment or a share.

https://probe2928-crypto.github.io/oriel/

## 한국어 (선택: 같은 날 짧게, 또는 댓글로)

2020년 태국, 2025년 아프리카 콩고 등에서 봉사하며 배웠습니다. 한 번의 큰 선물보다 꾸준한 작은 도움이 중요하고, 기부한 사람은 돈이 어디에 쓰였는지 볼 수 있어야 한다는 것. 오늘 그 배움으로 재단을 시작합니다.

오리엘 재단(明窗基金會)은 도움이 필요한 가정과 사랑스러운 아이들을 위해 준비 중인 재단입니다. 배움에서 시작해, 아이와 형편이 어려운 가정의 생활을 함께 돕고, 마을이 원할 때 교실과 학교를 짓습니다. 오리엘은 벽에서 내민, 빛을 더 들이는 창입니다. 배움에는 빛을, 기부에는 맑은 창을 내겠다는 뜻입니다.

모든 기부는 공개 블록체인에 기록하고, 모든 지출에 영수증을 붙입니다. 재단 등록을 마치기 전에는 기부를 받지 않습니다. 첫 현지 파트너, 교육·비영리 법률·회계 분야 자문, 창립 서포터를 찾고 있습니다.
